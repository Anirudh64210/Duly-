## Findings

1. **D32 is not true of the engine the app runs.** D32: a missing recommendation date "stops
   blocking once a filing date or an EAD is recorded". On the path engine (POST /v1/compute,
   the desktop app), a filed and approved card with no dso_on still asks for the date and
   makes the verdict Can't tell yet. A W2-P1 test asserts this, citing the founder decision of
   2026-10-01 in SPEC-W2 point 45. compute_f1 still follows D32 (test_f1_context passes). The
   SPEC's decision is about the last day. Applying it after the filing, when the last day no
   longer matters, overrides D32. Under CLAUDE.md, DECISIONS.md wins, so this goes to the
   founder (disputes).
2. **A negative hours figure is read as fact** (review finding 1, still open). It gives 121
   days, a limit day of 2026-08-29 and an at-or-over line, and nothing asks about it. No test
   enters a negative figure.
3. **The planning setting has no name of its own on screen** (review note).
   labels.ts SETTING_NAMES lacks plan.prepare_months, so nothing says it is Duly's setting,
   not law.
4. **Limit-day words are untested where they will be shown.** The engine gives none and no
   screen labels the item yet. The N2 rule about words holds only because no words exist.
5. SPEC-W2's Covers names no register row for the work of any single plan. The rows W2-001 to
   W2-006 now exist. W2-P2 and the later plans should name theirs.
