## Findings

1. **A trip entered wholly after today gets a conflict from a day nobody entered.** Trip
   2026-10-31 to 2026-11-15, today 2026-10-02: besides the two after_today questions (right),
   the engine asks trip.t1.ends_before_starts with `ends: 2026-10-02`, today, while the person
   entered 2026-11-15. plan-w2-p2-fix3 says a span's order conflict comes only from dates the
   person entered. The question says it withholds trip.t1, yet the absence window shows
   2026-10-31 to 2027-03-31 with no waits_on. The verdict is cannot_tell, so nothing reads as
   settled. G9 says a planned trip is a compute on a copy of the case, so the screen must not
   send one as a record until that exists.
2. **A rule check blanks a known confirmed day** (review finding 1, reproduced). A part-time
   pre-completion job at 25 hours, confirmed 2026-10-01: the check-in row is all null, with no
   reason. At 20 hours it reads due 2026-10-31.
3. **No test pins the count when the confirmed day conflicts** (review finding 2). Mutant N5
   survives tests/unit/engine and tests/holdout.
4. **Nothing before 2026-09-14 gets a trip or address date.** The rules' earliest saved copy is
   2026-09-14 (effective_from_basis earliest_saved_copy), so a move or a departure before that
   day is withheld with rule_copy_not_saved_for_event_day. That follows PRINCIPLES (no rule
   without its saved words), but every trip and move before that day shows no date and goes
   to the DSO. An older saved copy of 8 CFR 214.2 would date them.
5. **A withheld watch row has no waits_on** (review finding 4). It needs a contract change
   first.
6. **The address conflicts reach the screen as "Some facts entered do not fit together"**, and
   on Result.tsx as a question about dates (review finding 3). The check schema has no label.
7. **No screen reads watch[], checkins[] or the trip and address windows yet.** G8, G9, G10 and
   G17 are each half built: the engine half is here, the screens are W2's later plans.
