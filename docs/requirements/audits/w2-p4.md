   contract as of 444fa86 say keys.bin with no file in files/ gives a new empty index. The plan
   chose the contract; the reviewer agrees the rule is sound. Only the founder can amend D96 and
   the SPEC. I record DEC-D96-001 as partial until the three agree.

   no test would catch a change. This belongs to L-tests.

   L-tests.
