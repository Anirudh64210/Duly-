# Market Size, Demand Trends, and Acquisition Channels for a US International-Student Visa-Compliance Product (Duly)

## Key Question 1: Latest counts (total students, OPT, STEM OPT, CPT, new enrollment trend, India/China shares)

### Takeaway
The addressable population is roughly 1.18M international students (2024/25), of whom ~294K are on OPT (up 21% YoY, now 25% of the total), ~165K participated in STEM OPT, and ~131K got CPT authorizations in 2024. The stock is at a record high, but the inflow is falling: new enrollments dropped 7% in 2024/25 and fall 2025 snapshots show a ~17% decline in new starts, so the OPT/compliance segment keeps growing for 1-3 more years while the pipeline behind it shrinks.

### Cited Findings
- Total international students 2024/25: 1,177,766 (1.2M), +5% YoY, 6% of all US higher-ed enrollment — [IIE Open Doors 2025 press release](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/)
- OPT participants 2024/25: 294,253, +21% YoY — [IIE Open Doors 2025](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/)
- Academic levels 2024/25: graduate 488,481 (-3%), undergraduate 357,231 (+4%), OPT 294,253 (+21%) — [IIE Open Doors 2025](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/)
- India: 363,019 students (+10%); China: 265,919 (-4%); together ~55% of international enrollment — [IIE Open Doors 2025](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/)
- 57% of international students pursued STEM fields (Open Doors 2025) — [IIE Open Doors 2025](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/)
- New (first-time) enrollment 2024/25: -7% overall; new grad students -15%, new undergrads +4-5% — [IIE Open Doors 2025](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/); [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/11/there-were-more-international-students-in-the-us-than-ever-in-2024-25-but-commencements-are-declining/)
- 2024 SEVIS-derived figures (aggregated by Alma from CRS/SEVIS sources): 418,781 nonimmigrants gained OPT-based work authorization in 2024 (26% of F-1/M-1 students); 194,554 pre/post-completion OPT authorizations (+21.1%); 95,384 STEM OPT authorizations in 2024 (+54% vs 2023); 165,524 total STEM OPT participants in 2024; CPT authorizations 130,586 (-0.4%) — [Alma OPT statistics](https://tryalma.com/learn/opt-visa-statistics)
- STEM OPT nationality mix: India ~48% of STEM OPT participants, China ~20.4%; Asian nationals over two-thirds of the OPT-authorized population — [Alma OPT statistics](https://tryalma.com/learn/opt-visa-statistics)
- OPT participants now 25% of all international students, up from 16% ten years ago; ICEF projects continued ~14% OPT growth for 2025/26 — [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/11/there-were-more-international-students-in-the-us-than-ever-in-2024-25-but-commencements-are-declining/)
- Fall 2025 (2025/26 preview): NAFSA reports a "17 percent decline in new international student enrollment" and a 7% decline in total enrollment excluding OPT — [NAFSA Fall 2025 Snapshot](https://www.nafsa.org/fall-2025-international-student-enrollment-snapshot-economic-impact); [Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/new-international-enrollment-decline-fall-nafsa/806482/)
- Fall 2025 downturn cost: over $1.1B lost revenue and ~23,000 fewer jobs for 2025-26; international students contributed $43B to the US economy in 2024-25 — [NAFSA](https://www.nafsa.org/about/about-nafsa/international-students-contributed-43-billion-us-economy-2024-2025-fall-2025)
- Earlier NAFSA/JB International analysis projected a potential 150K fewer international students and ~$7B economic loss scenario for fall 2025 — [NAFSA](https://www.nafsa.org/about/about-nafsa/us-economy-could-suffer-7-billion-loss-precipitous-drop-international-students); [Higher Ed Dive](https://www.highereddive.com/news/international-student-decline-fall-2025-projection/756500/)

### Inferences
- Serviceable core market for a compliance/OPT tool today: ~294K on OPT + ~95-165K in STEM OPT flow + ~130K/yr CPT users, plus final-year students preparing OPT filings; realistically a 400-600K-person annual serviceable pool, ~50-70% of it Indian and Chinese nationals.
- OPT population lags enrollment by 2-4 years, so even with a 17% drop in new starts, the OPT segment likely keeps growing through ~2027 before contracting.
- Note the definitional split: "enrolled" (~883K excluding OPT) vs "OPT" (~294K); Duly's paying user is concentrated in the OPT/STEM OPT slice plus students within ~12 months of graduation.

### Gaps
- No official SEVIS "by the Numbers" 2025 full report figures were directly retrieved (ICE's latest posted PDF found was CY2023); 2024 SEVIS figures above come via Alma's aggregation of CRS/SEVIS sources and were not independently verified against the primary PDF.
- No point-in-time count of students currently on CPT (only annual authorization counts).

## Key Question 2: Demand-side signals (search volume, tool usage, TrackMyOPT as proxy)

### Takeaway
No reliable published keyword-volume numbers for terms like "OPT tracker" or "OPT unemployment days calculator" were found; the strongest quantified demand proxy is TrackMyOPT's own claims (3,000+ signups, 10,000+ regular users of its free tools) plus a dense ecosystem of competing free calculators (university ISS pages, GoElite, Sojourning Scholar, EduConnect) and active Blind/forum threads, indicating real recurring search demand around unemployment-day math and deadlines.

### Cited Findings
- TrackMyOPT claims "3,000+ users signed up" and "10,000+ people regularly use free tools"; pricing Free / $49.99-yr Pro / $149.99-yr Dedicated; features include unemployment-day counter, STEM extension calculator, USCIS case tracking, document vault, H-1B sponsor database — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/) (self-reported marketing claims, unverified)
- TrackMyOPT maintains a dedicated free "OPT Unemployment Days Calculator / 90-Day Rule Tracker" tool page and SEO blog posts on the 90-day rule, indicating they see search demand worth targeting — [TrackMyOPT tool](https://www.trackmyopt.com/dashboard/opt-tools/opt-clock); [TrackMyOPT blog](https://www.trackmyopt.com/blog/90-day-unemployment-rule-opt)
- Multiple independent free calculators/explainers compete for the same queries: GoElite, Sojourning Scholar OPT calculator, EduConnect USA (Aug 2026 post), VisaPro, plus university pages (ASU, CU Boulder) — [Sojourning Scholar](https://sojourningscholar.com/opt-calculator/); [GoElite](https://goelite.com/migration/en/knowledge-base/opt-work-unemployment-days-and-grace-period); [EduConnect](https://educonnectusa.com/2026/08/opt-unemployment-days/); [ASU ISSC](https://issc.asu.edu/news/calculating-your-opt-unemployment-days)
- Anxiety-driven peer discussion exists on Blind about OPT/STEM OPT unemployment days — [Blind thread](https://www.teamblind.com/post/optstem-opt-unemployment-days-q2xdgrkq)
- Spring 2025 SEVIS termination crisis: 4,700+ SEVIS records terminated and 1,600+ visas revoked as of May 7, 2025 under the "Student Criminal Alien Initiative"; courts found the actions "arbitrary, capricious, and not in accordance with the law" and terminations were reversed, but a replacement termination policy was announced — [National Immigration Forum explainer](https://forumtogether.org/article/explainer-revocation-of-student-visas-and-termination-of-sevis-records/); [Inside Higher Ed](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/global/international-students-us/2025/04/24/international-student-lawsuits-result-restored)

### Inferences
- The 2025 SEVIS termination episode is a strong demand catalyst: it made record-keeping and proof-of-compliance salient to hundreds of thousands of students overnight, which is exactly Duly's WorkBook value proposition.
- TrackMyOPT's ~3K paid-funnel signups vs ~10K free-tool users suggests a free-tool-first SEO/utility funnel works, but conversion to paid is the hard part; its $50/yr price point is a useful anchor.
- Free-tool competition (universities and blogs give calculators away) means the calculator itself is a channel, not a product; the wedge has to be ongoing tracking + records.

### Gaps
- No published keyword-volume data (Ahrefs/Semrush/Google Trends write-ups) found for "OPT tracker", "I-983 example", "SEVIS terminated what to do" etc.; volumes would need direct keyword-tool access.
- TrackMyOPT numbers are self-reported on its own site; no third-party verification, funding, or revenue data found.

## Key Question 3: Channel viability (university ISS/DSO endorsements, student groups, creators)

### Takeaway
There is strong precedent for universities formally recommending or licensing third-party tools to international students: Sprintax claims 650 school/corporate partners with ISS offices distributing codes (Columbia, WashU, Buffalo, BGSU), and Interstride sells institution-paid licenses adopted by Brown, Lehigh, Wesleyan, Cincinnati and others. ISO insurance shows a parallel student-org channel (e.g., an exclusive deal with a university's Indian student association). WhatsApp/Telegram cohort groups for Indian students are large, organized, and openly indexed, making them a viable bootstrapped channel.

### Cited Findings
- Sprintax reports "650 School & corporate partners" with a dedicated account manager model; partnership terms are custom (demo-based sales, no published pricing) — [Sprintax higher-ed partners](https://www.sprintax.com/partners/higher-education/)
- Universities actively route students to Sprintax: Columbia ISSO, WashU OISS, University at Buffalo (2026 announcement), BGSU all host Sprintax instructions/access pages — [Columbia ISSO](https://isso.columbia.edu/content/filing-your-non-resident-tax-forms-using-sprintax-f-and-j); [WashU OISS](https://oiss.washu.edu/sprintax-returns/); [UB](https://www.buffalo.edu/administrative-services/managing-people/managing-hr.host.html/content/shared/www/administrative-services/news/2026/sprintax-now-available.detail.html)
- Interstride is an institution-paid platform ("Get a Demo" sales motion, no public pricing) with named adopters incl. University of Cincinnati, Quinnipiac, Baruch, Westcliff, Babson, UT Arlington; Brown's career center launched an Interstride portal in 2024; Lehigh and Wesleyan career centers list it as a partnership resource — [Interstride](https://www.interstride.com/); [Interstride case study](https://www.interstride.com/blog/how-to-successfully-launch-interstride-and-onboard-international-students/); [Brown Daily Herald](https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/2024/10/interstride-portal-to-help-international-students-at-brown-find-employment-opportunities); [Lehigh](https://careercenter.lehigh.edu/international-studentsoiss-partnership)
- ISO Student Health Insurance announced an "Exclusive Partnership" with the University of South Florida Students of India Association (student-org channel, not the university itself), and maintains a universities/affiliates links page; ISO was acquired by NSM Insurance Group in 2024 — [ISO blog](https://www.isoa.org/blog/iso-announces-exclusive-partnership-with-the-university-of-south-florida-students-of-india-association); [ISO affiliates](https://www.isoa.org/universities_and_schools); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/nsm-insurance-group-acquires-iso-student-health-insurance-302209295.html)
- Indian-student WhatsApp/Telegram infrastructure is large and publicly indexed: WeMakeScholars runs "MS in US 2026" WhatsApp groups and lists 70+ study-abroad groups; BharathRise and others aggregate US/Canada/UK groups; Telegram has open "Indian Students Community USA" channels — [WeMakeScholars](https://www.wemakescholars.com/blog/whatsapp-group-for-ms-in-us-fall); [WeMakeScholars 70+ groups](https://www.wemakescholars.com/blog/70-study-abroad-whatsapp-groups-for-students); [BharathRise](https://bharathrise.com/knowledge-hub/whatsapp-groups); [Telegram channel](https://t.me/IndianStudentsCommunity)
- TrackMyOPT's marketing name-drops students at CMU, Georgia Tech, USC, NYU, Columbia etc., suggesting organic student-level (not institutional) adoption — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/) (self-reported)

### Inferences
- Two proven channel archetypes: (a) institution-paid license (Interstride model, long sales cycle, custom pricing, requires demos and pilots) and (b) ISS-recommended student-paid tool with discount codes (Sprintax model). For a bootstrapped startup, (b) plus student-org exclusives (ISO's USF-SIA playbook) is more reachable than enterprise sales.
- Caution: DSOs are conservative about endorsing compliance advice from third parties (liability); Sprintax succeeded in tax (adjacent, low DSO liability), so positioning Duly as record-keeping rather than legal advice matters for ISS acceptance.
- Cohort WhatsApp groups form annually around admission cycles ("MS in US Fall 2026"), giving a predictable seasonal acquisition calendar aligned with I-20/visa/OPT milestones.

### Gaps
- No public data on what universities pay Interstride or Sprintax per contract (both are demo/custom-priced); no case study with contract dollar figures found.
- YouTube/creator ecosystem around OPT content (channel sizes, reach of creators like immigration YouTubers) was not quantified within tool budget; no reliable subscriber/view data collected. Needs a follow-up pass.

## Key Question 4: Churn/lifecycle math (years in F-1 → OPT → STEM → H-1B pipeline)

### Takeaway
The structural LTV window is long for this category: a master's student has ~2 years enrolled + up to 12 months OPT + 24 months STEM OPT, i.e. a 3-5 year usable relationship, and ~34% of first-time H-1B holders come from student visas, with FY2026 H-1B selection around 35%, meaning most users cycle through multiple lottery years while still needing F-1 compliance tracking.

### Cited Findings
- Post-completion OPT is 12 months; STEM OPT adds 24 months (36 months total possible), and timely STEM OPT applicants get an automatic 180-day extension of work authorization — [DHS Study in the States](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/sevis-help-hub/student-records/fm-student-employment/f-1-stem-optional-practical-training-opt)
- 34% of first-time H-1B workers transition from student visas — [Alma OPT statistics](https://tryalma.com/learn/opt-visa-statistics)
- FY2026 H-1B lottery: roughly 35% of eligible beneficiaries selected (selection rate rose vs prior years as registrations fell) — [Ogletree](https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/h-1b-registration-numbers-for-fy-2026-h-1b-cap-reveal-an-increase-in-selection-rate/); [Day1CPT blog](https://day1cptuniversities.com/blog/35-were-selected-fy2026-h1b-lottery)
- 56-57% of international students are in STEM fields, i.e. eligible for the 36-month OPT track — [IIE Open Doors 2025](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/); [Alma](https://tryalma.com/learn/opt-visa-statistics)

### Inferences
- Realistic paying-lifecycle: ~6-12 months pre-OPT (application anxiety window) + 12 months OPT + 24 months STEM OPT ≈ 3.5-4 years for a STEM master's user; non-STEM users churn after ~18 months. At TrackMyOPT-style $50/yr pricing, structural LTV ceiling is roughly $150-200 per STEM user before H-1B/status change churn.
- With ~35% annual H-1B selection, a STEM OPT user typically gets 2-3 lottery attempts while still on F-1 — the H-1B transition itself (not dissatisfaction) is the dominant churn event, which argues for expanding the product past F-1 (H-1B/green-card tracking) to extend LTV.

### Gaps
- No authoritative published figure for "average total years on F-1 including OPT"; the 3-5 year window above is derived from program rules, not measured cohort data.

## Key Question 5: Headwinds (policy risk to OPT, enrollment decline, TAM over 3-5 years)

### Takeaway
Policy risk is the dominant TAM threat: DHS finalized a rule (published July 17, 2026, effective ~mid-September 2026) replacing duration-of-status with fixed 4-year admissions and 30-day grace periods, a $100K OPT fee was under White House consideration as of Aug 2026 (with a DHS OPT rule slated for Feb 2027), and a House bill (H.R. 2315) proposes eliminating OPT outright. Perversely, every one of these increases the compliance burden and anxiety Duly monetizes in the near term, while the elimination scenarios (fee or repeal) would gut the core market if enacted.

### Cited Findings
- DHS final rule eliminates duration of status for F/J/I: F-1s admitted for fixed periods up to 4 years, grace period cut from 60 to 30 days, extensions of stay via USCIS required, unlawful presence begins accruing immediately when the admission period expires; published July 17, 2026, effective ~60 days later (mid-Sept 2026) — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-dhs-issues-final-rule-replacing-admission-for-duration-of-status-with-fixed-periods-of-stay-for-f-j-and-i-nonimmigrants.html); [Ogletree](https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/dhs-publishes-final-rule-replacing-duration-of-status-with-fixed-admission-periods-for-f-j-and-i-nonimmigrants/) (the Aug 2025 NPRM preceded this; see [BU ISSO](https://www.bu.edu/isso/2025/09/29/dhs-seeks-to-replace-d-s-with-a-fixed-expiration-date/))
- Under the final rule, timely STEM OPT applicants still get the automatic 180-day extension, and F-1s with pending OPT at the effective date have transition accommodations — [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-dhs-issues-final-rule-replacing-admission-for-duration-of-status-with-fixed-periods-of-stay-for-f-j-and-i-nonimmigrants.html)
- $100,000 OPT fee: reported by WSJ as under Trump administration consideration (July-Aug 2026); DHS regulatory agenda lists Feb 2027 for an OPT rule; a similar $100K H-1B fee was struck down by a federal court in June 2026; unclear whether the White House will proceed — [Forbes, Stuart Anderson](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stuartanderson/2026/08/03/new-immigration-plan-considered-to-stop-students-from-working-in-us/); [EIG analysis](https://eig.org/100000-opt-fee-h-1b-abuse/)
- H.R. 2315 "Fairness for High-Skilled Americans Act of 2025" (Rep. Gosar, 119th Congress) would eliminate OPT; introduced April 2025, no evidence of passage — [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/2315/text); [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2025/04/08/new-bill-proposed-could-end-opt-for-international-students-in-us/)
- Enrollment headwinds compounding: fall 2025 new enrollment -17% (NAFSA), commencements -7% in 2024/25, and Forbes reports survey evidence of students "moving away" from US colleges (Nov 2025) — [NAFSA snapshot](https://www.nafsa.org/fall-2025-international-student-enrollment-snapshot-economic-impact); [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/2025/11/there-were-more-international-students-in-the-us-than-ever-in-2024-25-but-commencements-are-declining/); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/michaeltnietzel/2025/11/25/new-survey-international-students-are-moving-away-from-us-colleges/)
- Presidents' Alliance flagged "worrying trends" in SEVIS data amid policy uncertainty (2025) — [Presidents' Alliance](https://www.presidentsalliance.org/press/new-sevis-data-points-towards-worrying-trends-in-u-s-international-student-enrollment-amid-policy-uncertainty-presidents-alliance-warns/)

### Inferences
- 3-5 year TAM shape: base case, OPT population peaks ~2026-2027 (still riding the record 2023-24 intake) then declines 10-25% as the smaller 2025-26 cohorts graduate; bear case (OPT fee enacted and survives courts, or H.R. 2315-style repeal) collapses the paid segment; bull case for Duly specifically, the D/S final rule creates brand-new recurring compliance work (extension-of-stay filings, hard expiration dates, immediate unlawful-presence accrual) that did not exist before and directly matches a deadline-tracking product.
- Near-term (12-24 months), policy chaos is a demand tailwind for compliance tooling even as it shrinks the long-term market; product positioning should hedge by covering the whole F-1→H-1B lifecycle rather than OPT alone.
- Regulatory changes force annual product updates; the same volatility that creates demand also raises content-maintenance cost and the liability stakes of being wrong.

### Gaps
- No NAFSA fall 2026 projection document found (searches surfaced fall 2025 outlook/snapshot only); forward projections beyond 2025-26 are inference, not published forecasts.
- Litigation status of the D/S final rule (whether challenged/enjoined after its ~Sept 2026 effective date) was not confirmed within tool budget.
