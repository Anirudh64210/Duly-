# Competitive and Monetization Landscape: Consumer Immigration/Compliance Tools for International Students and Early-Career Visa Holders (US, 2025-2026)

All prices verified from live pages fetched 2026-09-28 unless a different date is noted.

## Key Question 1: Per-product pricing, model, free tier, and traction

### Takeaway
The market splits into three price bands: cheap consumer trackers ($15/mo or $50-155/yr subscriptions: TrackMyOPT, Lawfully, VisaWatch, FrogHire), mid-priced transactional filing services ($699-$2,399 one-time: Boundless, SelfLawyer), and high-ticket flat-fee legal ($3,000-$25,000 per case: Alma, Deel/Legalpad, Casium). Nobody sells a student-priced compliance/evidence subscription; Interstride is B2B (university-paid, free to students).

### Cited Findings

**TrackMyOPT** (closest direct competitor)
- Free plan (forever): OPT filing calculator, timeline dashboard, unemployment day tracker, STEM extension calculator, manual USCIS case refresh — [TrackMyOPT homepage](https://www.trackmyopt.com/) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- Pro: $49.99/yr billed annually ($4.17/mo effective), with "introductory rate of $0.99 for 7 days"; adds daily 9 AM email reminders, automatic daily USCIS checks, status alerts — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/); pricing page exists at [trackmyopt.com/pricing](https://www.trackmyopt.com/pricing)
- Dedicated: $149.99/yr ($12.50/mo effective); adds priority support and one complimentary 60-minute attorney consultation — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/)
- Feature set: OPT timeline tracking, 90-day unemployment clock, USCIS case monitoring, H-1B sponsor database (25,000+ employers), job-search CRM, AI resume tool, Chrome extension — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/)
- Traction claims (self-reported): "3,000+ users have signed up" and "10,000+ people regularly use our free tools" — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/)

**Lawfully** (USCIS case tracker)
- Free to download; premium tiers as App Store in-app purchases: Lawfully Premium $14.99/mo or $29.99-$34.99 per 3 months; Case Pro Unlimited $14.99/mo, $29.99/3-months, or $154.99/yr; also one-off "gem" purchases $9.99-$39.99 — [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lawfully-case-tracker/id1435063223) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- Traction: 4.8 stars, ~173,000 App Store ratings; claims 2.5M+ users; community with 200K+ posts — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lawfully-case-tracker/id1435063223)
- Funding: closed $2.5M seed in Nov 2022, at which point it reported 800K registered cases on the app — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/11/02/2546356/0/en/Lawfully-Closes-2-5M-in-Seed-Funding-Hits-800k-Registered-Cases-on-its-App.html); [Law360](https://www.law360.com/pulse/articles/1545873/immigration-app-lawfully-raises-2-5m-in-early-funding)
- lawfully.com/pricing returned 404 at fetch time; pricing lives in-app, not on a web pricing page.

**Sprintax** (nonresident tax filing; the benchmark student-paid immigration-adjacent SaaS)
- Retail prices (no school code): Federal 1040NR prep $54.95; State prep $49.95 per state; State-only return $59.95; Form 8843 $19.95; ITIN (W-7) $19.95; FICA (Form 843) $19.95; post-filing support $29.95 — [Sprintax FAQ](https://taxprep.sprintax.com/faq.html) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- Distribution model: universities buy codes; e.g., Columbia offers Sprintax federal filing at no cost to students, state return at a discounted individual fee of $44.95 — [Columbia ISSO](https://isso.columbia.edu/content/filing-your-non-resident-tax-forms-using-sprintax-f-and-j) (2025 tax year); WashU "covers NRA fees for filing the U.S. federal tax return and Form 8843" — [WashU OISS](https://oiss.washu.edu/sprintax-returns/)

**Interstride**
- B2B model: sold to universities as an institutional platform (career + immigration resources for international students); students access free via their school; e.g., positioned as a career-center resource at Binghamton, USC, Pitt, IU, UConn — [Interstride career centers page](https://www.interstride.com/career-centers/); [Binghamton listing](https://careertools.binghamton.edu/resources/interstride/); [Pitt](https://www.gradstudies.pitt.edu/professional-development/interstride)
- No public per-university or per-student pricing found (institutional sales, quote-based). Gap noted below.

**Boundless**
- 2026 service fees (per third-party review, published 2026-02-03, updated 2026-04-27): K-1 only $699 Essential / $1,349 Premium; Marriage AOS or CR-1/IR-1 consular $749 Essential / $1,249 Premium; K-1 + AOS bundle $1,349 Essential / $2,399 Premium; government fees ($1,455-$4,740) separate — [Ready for Visa review](https://www.readyforvisa.com/blog/boundless-immigration-review) (note: this is a competitor-adjacent review site; Boundless's own site does not publish service fees on its costs page, only USCIS government fees — [Boundless costs guide](https://www.boundless.com/guides/topics/costs-and-fees))
- One-time transactional fees with payment plans offered — [Boundless payment plan](https://www.boundless.com/payment-plan)
- Early funding: $7.8M raise (2019) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/03/28/boundless-gets-7-8m-to-help-immigrants-navigate-the-convoluted-green-card-process/amp/)
- Boundless is family/marriage-focused; not a student compliance product.

**SelfLawyer**
- One-time flat-fee model ("You will pay your legal fees only once"); family-based services (marriage green card, K-1, citizenship, removal of conditions); exact prices NOT published on site, quote on contact; claims "99% approval rate" and submission to USCIS within 7 business days of documents — [self-lawyer.com](https://self-lawyer.com/) (fetched 2026-09-28; selflawyer.com redirects here)

**Alma (tryalma.com)**

Includes RFE/NOID response and one free refile if denied; USCIS fees excluded. 50/50 payment plan for business clients — [Alma pricing](https://www.tryalma.com/pricing) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- Targets employers and high-skill individuals, not students on a budget.

**F1 Hire → FrogHire.ai**
- F1Hire shut down as a brand: site states "F1Hire is no longer maintained" and redirects to FrogHire.ai — [f1hire.com/pricing](https://www.f1hire.com/pricing) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- FrogHire has Free, Premium (paid subscription), and Institutional (via school career centers) plans; exact prices are gated behind the logged-in app (app.froghire.ai/pricing) and were not retrievable; referral program grants "14+ days of Premium" per paid referral — [FrogHire FAQ](https://www.froghire.ai/help/faq)
- Distribution mirrors Interstride: listed as a career-center resource at Binghamton and USC — [Binghamton](https://careertools.binghamton.edu/resources/froghire-ai-formerly-f1hire/); [USC](https://careers.usc.edu/resources/frog-hire/)
- Positioning: "A Must-Have Tool For OPT/CPT/H-1B Job Seekers" (job-search/sponsor-filtering, not compliance) — [froghire.ai](https://www.froghire.ai/)

**VisaNation**
- VisaNation Law Group (immi-usa.com) is a law firm with content-marketing pages on visa fees; no public self-service consumer tool pricing found in searches — [VisaNation H-1B fees page](https://www.immi-usa.com/h1b-visa-application-filing-fees/). Gap noted below.

No consumer "ImmigrationOS" pricing exists to report; treat the term as reputationally loaded. (No reliable consumer-product source found; see Gaps.)

**Argo Visa** (ex-visa-officer interview prep)
- One-time services: DS-160 filing service $125 (basic/first-time cases); consultations Basic $479 (30 min) / Standard $639 (45 min) / Premium $789 (60 min) with Former Visa Officers; +$50 express within 48 hrs; DS-160 + consultation bundles — [Argo Visa services](https://www.argovisa.com/our-service) (fetched 2026-09-28)

**Legalpad / Deel Immigration**
- Legalpad acquired by Deel (2022); now operates as Deel Immigration, expanded from employer-sponsored to individual applicants ("Deel Mobility is now open to all companies and workers") — [Deel blog: Legalpad joins Deel](https://www.deel.com/blog/legalpad-joins-deel/); [Deel blog: open to individuals](https://www.deel.com/blog/deel-immigration-open-to-individuals/)
- Public per-case consumer pricing not published; enterprise/quote-based — [Deel employee immigration](https://www.deel.com/hr-services/employee-immigration/). Gap noted below.

**Casium** (recent AI entrant, employment-based)
- Seattle startup founded by ex-Microsoft ML scientist Priyanka Kulkarni (launched 2024); AI eligibility assessment, visa-category recommendation (H-1B, O-1, EB-1A), document automation with attorney coordination; $5M seed; pricing not disclosed — [NRI Pulse](https://nripulse.com/tech-scientist-turns-visa-struggles-into-ai-startup-aimed-at-simplifying-us-immigration/) (article Oct 2025)

**VisaWatch** (recent tracker entrant)
- USCIS tracker app using the official USCIS Torch API; free tier "up to 3 cases," 7-day Premium trial, Premium = unlimited family cases, no ads, receipt numbers stored locally; exact dollar pricing not shown on comparison page; explicitly positions against Lawfully ("Lawfully typically scales by number of cases") — [VisaWatch vs Lawfully](https://www.visawatch.app/vs/lawfully)

### Inferences
- The consumer-tracker price ceiling students actually pay appears to be roughly $50-$155/yr (TrackMyOPT Pro $49.99/yr; Lawfully Case Pro $154.99/yr). $14.99/mo monthly pricing exists (Lawfully) but is oriented at anxious one-time case-trackers (green card applicants), not multi-year students.
- Attorney-consult bundling is the established upsell to justify a >$100/yr tier (TrackMyOPT Dedicated includes a 60-min consult at $149.99/yr).
- Everyone monetizing students directly is either job-search (FrogHire) or tracker (TrackMyOPT); the compliance/evidence layer is unpriced territory.

## Key Question 2: Peace-of-mind subscriptions vs transactional filings; what works for students

### Takeaway
Subscriptions monetize ongoing anxiety (case trackers, deadline clocks) at $50-155/yr; transactional one-time fees monetize discrete filings at $699-$10,000+. For students specifically, the only proven direct-pay model observed is the cheap annual subscription with a generous free tier and university/career-center distribution as an alternative channel.

### Cited Findings
- Subscription (peace of mind): TrackMyOPT $49.99-$149.99/yr — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/); Lawfully $14.99/mo tiers — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lawfully-case-tracker/id1435063223); VisaWatch freemium premium — [VisaWatch](https://www.visawatch.app/vs/lawfully)
- Transactional: Boundless $699-$2,399 per filing — [Ready for Visa](https://www.readyforvisa.com/blog/boundless-immigration-review); Alma $3,000-$25,000 flat per case — [Alma pricing](https://www.tryalma.com/pricing); Argo $125-$789 one-time — [Argo](https://www.argovisa.com/our-service); Sprintax ~$55-$105 per tax season one-time — [Sprintax FAQ](https://taxprep.sprintax.com/faq.html)
- B2B2C (university pays, student free): Interstride — [Interstride career centers](https://www.interstride.com/career-centers/); FrogHire Institutional plan — [FrogHire FAQ](https://www.froghire.ai/help/faq); Sprintax school codes covering federal filing — [Columbia ISSO](https://isso.columbia.edu/content/filing-your-non-resident-tax-forms-using-sprintax-f-and-j)
- TrackMyOPT's $0.99/7-day intro offer indicates conversion friction at even $49.99/yr for students — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/)

### Inferences
- Students have annual, recurring, calendar-driven compliance events (OPT filing windows, STEM extension, reporting deadlines, tax season), which structurally favors an annual subscription over per-filing fees; incumbents doing per-filing (Boundless, Alma) skip students because the student filings (I-765) are low-ticket.
- The B2B2C university channel is the proven zero-CAC route to this audience (Interstride, FrogHire, Sprintax all use it); a consumer-only paid app fights against "my DSO does this for free" perception.

### Gaps
- No public conversion/retention data for any of these products; no source states what fraction of TrackMyOPT or Lawfully users pay.

## Key Question 3: App-store evidence for visa-tracker apps

### Takeaway
Lawfully dominates the category (4.8 stars, ~173K ratings, 2.5M+ users claimed); praise centers on predictions and community, complaints on stale data ("app still did not update" after approval) and refresh reliability. Newer rivals differentiate on official-API freshness, no ads, and flat family pricing.

### Cited Findings
- Lawfully: 4.8/5, ~173,000 ratings; praised for accurate case-status predictions, multi-case tracking, community (200K+ posts); complaints: "Case got approved like 5 days ago, app still did not update", outdated processing-time data, misaligned prediction averages, questioned refresh function — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lawfully-case-tracker/id1435063223) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- Lawfully monetization visible in-app: $14.99/mo subscriptions plus $9.99-$39.99 consumable "gems" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lawfully-case-tracker/id1435063223)
- User skepticism threads exist ("LAWFULLY app, worth it?", "is Lawfully app safe?") on VisaJourney forums — [VisaJourney thread](https://www.visajourney.com/forums/topic/761414-lawfully-app-worth-it/); [safety thread](https://www.visajourney.com/forums/topic/772228-is-lawfully-app-safe/) (not fetched; thread titles indicate trust/price-worthiness debate)
- VisaWatch attacks Lawfully on ads, per-case pricing, and data provenance (official USCIS Torch API, receipt numbers stored locally) — [VisaWatch](https://www.visawatch.app/vs/lawfully)
- Trustpilot shows ~4,657 Lawfully reviews (count visible in localized Trustpilot listings) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lawfully.com)

### Inferences
- The recurring complaint pattern (stale USCIS data, ads, per-case fees) defines the trust bar a new student app must clear: fresh official data, transparent flat pricing, privacy-respecting storage.
- TrackMyOPT has no comparable app-store footprint found (web-first product), suggesting the OPT-specific niche has no dominant mobile app yet.

## Key Question 4: What is conspicuously NOT offered

### Takeaway
Across all products examined, no one offers: a versioned document vault with immigration-grade evidence chains, automatic work-record keeping for RFE/audit defense, personalized checklists with legal citations, or honest impact-scored policy news for students. The closest overlaps are TrackMyOPT (timelines/clocks, no evidence layer) and Lawfully (case status, no documents at all).

### Cited Findings
- TrackMyOPT's full feature list (timelines, unemployment clock, USCIS monitoring, sponsor DB, job CRM, resume AI, Chrome extension) contains no document vault, no work-evidence log, no cited checklists, no policy-news product — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/)
- Lawfully is case-status tracking + community + attorney referrals; no document storage or compliance record-keeping in its App Store feature description — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lawfully-case-tracker/id1435063223)
- VisaWatch deliberately narrows to "your USCIS case, tracked" — single-function, explicitly not a compliance suite — [VisaWatch](https://www.visawatch.app/vs/lawfully)
- Alma includes "compliance tracking" only as part of attorney-managed corporate cases at $3,500+ per case — [Alma pricing](https://www.tryalma.com/pricing)
- FrogHire/Interstride are job-search and career platforms, not compliance/evidence tools — [FrogHire](https://www.froghire.ai/); [Interstride](https://www.interstride.com/students/)
- Argo monetizes interview anxiety with human ex-officers, one-time, no software continuity — [Argo](https://www.argovisa.com/our-service)

### Inferences
- The specific Duly bundle (vault + versioning + auto work-records for RFE evidence + cited checklists + impact-scored updates) has no direct competitor at student price points; the nearest threat vectors are (a) TrackMyOPT expanding downstream from timelines, and (b) university-distributed platforms (Interstride) adding compliance modules.
- "Evidence for STEM OPT reporting/RFE" is a genuinely unclaimed wedge: incumbent trackers watch USCIS's state, none record the student's own compliance state.

### Gaps
- Absence-of-feature claims are based on public feature lists and app descriptions; logged-in product internals (FrogHire Premium, Lawfully Premium screens) could not be inspected.

## Key Question 5: Adjacent benchmarks — what students already pay

### Takeaway
International students already carry recurring costs of roughly $41-$81/mo for OPT insurance, ~$55-$105/season for Sprintax, and $36-$240/yr for study SaaS; a compliance subscription in the $30-$100/yr band sits comfortably inside demonstrated willingness to pay.

### Cited Findings
- Sprintax retail: federal $54.95 + state $49.95 (≈$105/season without school code); Form 8843 alone $19.95 — [Sprintax FAQ](https://taxprep.sprintax.com/faq.html) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- ISO OPT health insurance: OPTima Basic $41/mo, OPTima Enhanced $81/mo (Aetna PPO network) — [ISO OPT plans](https://www.isoa.org/opt_plans) (fetched 2026-09-28)
- Quizlet Plus: $35.99/yr ($2.99/mo effective); Plus Unlimited $44.99/yr — [StudyGuides.com](https://studyguides.com/articles/how-much-is-quizlet-plus-pricing-plans-and-free-alternatives) (2026 pricing)
- Chegg Study ~$15.95/mo; Chegg Study Pack ~$19.95/mo (2025 pricing) — [MyEngineeringBuddy](https://www.myengineeringbuddy.com/blog/chegg-reviews-alternatives-pricing-offerings-in-2025/)
- TrackMyOPT already anchors the OPT-compliance category at $49.99/yr — [TrackMyOPT](https://www.trackmyopt.com/)

### Inferences
- Calibration: students accept ~$3-$4/mo (annualized) for study tools, ~$50/yr for OPT tooling, ~$100/season for a must-do compliance task (taxes), and ~$500-$1,000/yr for mandatory insurance. A Duly price of $4-$8/mo annual (roughly $49-$99/yr), with a high-stakes tier near TrackMyOPT's $149.99 attorney-bundled tier, matches observed anchors.
- Sprintax proves students pay when the task is mandatory and mistakes are scary; insurance proves triple-digit annual spend is normal when status depends on it. Framing Duly as status protection (not productivity) aligns it with the higher-anchor category.

### Gaps
- Notion student pricing not separately verified this session (Notion has historically offered free Education Plus for students; unverified here, excluded from findings).

## Key Question 6: Recent entrants (2025-2026) in AI immigration copilots

### Takeaway
2024-2025 AI entrants (Casium, CaseBlink, plus attorney-side tools) target employers and attorneys with high-ticket employment visas; no funded 2025-2026 entrant was found selling an AI immigration copilot subscription to students, leaving that slot open.

### Cited Findings
- Casium (Seattle, founded 2024, $5M seed, ex-Microsoft founder): AI eligibility assessment + document automation + attorney coordination for H-1B/O-1/EB-1A; claims prep time cut "from several months to less than 10 business days"; pricing undisclosed — [NRI Pulse, Oct 2025](https://nripulse.com/tech-scientist-turns-visa-struggles-into-ai-startup-aimed-at-simplifying-us-immigration/)
- CaseBlink appears in CB Insights as an AI immigration company (attorney/case-prep side); details/pricing not retrievable this session — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/caseblink)
- VisaWatch (tracker, USCIS Torch API, freemium) is the newest consumer-side entrant found; not AI-copilot positioned — [VisaWatch](https://www.visawatch.app/vs/lawfully)
- F1Hire's 2025 rebrand to FrogHire.ai added AI framing ("Turbocharge your job search process 10 times faster") but remains a job-search tool — [froghire.ai](https://www.froghire.ai/)
- Alma publishes comparison SEO against Deel, Lawfully, Boundless, Visalaw.ai, Lighthouse — evidence the AI-legal segment is crowding at the employer tier — [Alma vs Lawfully](https://www.tryalma.com/learn/alma-vs-lawfully)

### Inferences
- The "AI copilot" wave is attacking the $3K-$10K attorney fee, not the $50/yr student wallet; students are being served AI only as resume/job-search features (FrogHire, TrackMyOPT's AI Resume Doctor).
- A student-priced AI scenario/checklist product would compete on trust and citations, where generic chatbots and job boards make no compliance claims.

### Gaps
- Product Hunt-specific 2025-2026 launches in this niche could not be enumerated (searches surfaced Microsoft Copilot noise instead); a dedicated Product Hunt crawl would be needed.
- No pricing found for Casium or CaseBlink.
