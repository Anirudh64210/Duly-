Scenarios
Ask anything. Duly answers from your case and the rules, cites them, and says when it isn't sure.
I lose my job I travel abroad My filing gets an RFE
You'd have 78 days, and clear moves.
If employment ends Nov 1: 12 of 90 unemployment days used, new work reported by Jan 18, 2027. Your STEM window stays open.
1
Land the next role first. Any qualifying work stops the clock: full-time, part-time at 20+ hours, contract, or self-employment in your field.
2
Report the change in the SEVP portal within 10 days: the end of the old job and the start of the new one.
3
If the search runs long, unpaid work in your field at 20+ hours legitimately stops the clock too. Real work, logged in WorkBook, not a paper shield.
Honest caveats: volunteering must be genuinely unpaid, in your field, and not displace paid labor. Several part-time jobs can add up to 20 hours. Some DSOs read these rules more strictly, so confirm with yours.

Travel is fine, with four things in hand.
While employed on approved OPT, you can leave and re-enter. Duly checked your four against the vault.
Passport valid through Mar 2031
F-1 visa stamp valid until Jul 12, 2028
EAD card in the vault
I-20 travel signature valid until Feb 14. After that, ask your DSO for a fresh one
On the way back, pull a fresh I-94 and check it. Duly reminds you and files it.
Honest caveats: days abroad while unemployed still count toward your 90. If a STEM filing is pending, wait for the receipt notice before flying, because re-entry on a pending case is riskier and we'd rather say so.

An RFE is a request, not a denial.
USCIS is asking for evidence, and you keep working while you answer. Deadlines are shorter now under PA-2026-05.
1
Read exactly what's asked. Answer those requests, in their order, nothing invented.
2
One complete response, sent once. Duly assembles the pack: notice on top, exhibit index, numbered exhibits, continuous pages.
3
A missing document gets explained, never omitted, and your consistency check runs across every exhibit before it ships.
Honest caveats: Duly organizes the record; the cover letter and any legal argument come from you or an attorney. For a complex request, an attorney review is money well spent.
