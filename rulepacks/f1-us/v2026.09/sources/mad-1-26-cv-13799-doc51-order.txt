PRELIMINARY INJUNCTION ORDER
POSTPONING EFFECTIVE DATE OF FINAL RULE
Pursuant to § 705 of the Administrative Procedure Act and Fed. R. Civ. P. 65, after a
hearing, and for the reasons set forth in the Court’s Memorandum and Order, filed herewith,
Plaintiffs’ Motion for APA § 705 Relief, a Preliminary Injunction or, Alternatively, Summary
Judgment, is GRANTED in part and DENIED in part, as follows:
1. For purposes of this Order,
a. the term “Final Rule” shall mean the Final Rule entitled “Establishing a Fixed
Time Period of Admission and an Extension of Stay Procedure for
Nonimmigrant Academic Students, Exchange Visitors, and Representatives of
Foreign Information Media,” published at 91 Fed. Reg. 44976 (July 17, 2026);
and
b. the term “Defendants” shall mean the United States Department of Homeland
Security, United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement, Markwayne
Mullin, in his official capacity as Secretary of Homeland Security, and David
Case 1:26-cv-13799-FDS Document 51 Filed 09/14/26 Page 2 of 2
Venturella, in his official capacity as Acting Director of United States
Immigration and Customs Enforcement.
2. Plaintiffs’ Motion to Stay Under 5 U.S.C. 705 is GRANTED to the extent that it
seeks to postpone the effective date of the Final Rule pursuant to the
Administrative Procedure Act, 5 U.S.C. § 705.
3. The effective date of the Final Rule is hereby POSTPONED pending further order
of this court or resolution of this proceeding on the merits.
4. Defendants are preliminarily enjoined from taking any further action to
implement the Final Rule pending further order of this court or resolution of this
proceeding on the merits.
5. To the extent that plaintiffs seek vacatur of the Final Rule, summary judgment in
their favor, or other relief, the motion is DENIED without prejudice to its
renewal.
So Ordered.
