**Layout.** The header card reads "Help Center" with "Everything you need to know about OPT and TrackMyOPT". Three pill tabs sit below it: **OPT Guide** (active), App Features, FAQ.

**Content.**

- **"What is OPT?"**: "Optional Practical Training (OPT) is a temporary employment authorization that allows F-1 visa students to work in the United States for up to 12 months after completing their academic program. OPT must be directly related to your major area of study." Two boxes follow:
  - **Pre-Completion OPT:** "Work authorization while still enrolled in school. Limited to 20 hours/week during classes, full-time during breaks. Time used reduces post-completion OPT."
  - **Post-Completion OPT:** "12-month work authorization after graduation. Must apply within 60 days of program end date. Most common type of OPT that TrackMyOPT helps you manage."
- **"OPT Timeline & Key Dates"**, a vertical timeline with five colour-coded steps:
  1. **Apply for OPT**: "90 days before to 60 days after program end". "Submit Form I-765 with required documents to USCIS. File as early as possible within this window."
  2. **Program End Date**: "Your graduation/completion date". "This is your official program end date as indicated on your I-20. Grace period and OPT timing are calculated from this date."
  3. **60-Day Grace Period**: "Starts after program end". "You have 60 days to either start OPT, transfer schools, change status, or depart the US. This is NOT additional OPT time."
  4. **OPT Start Date**: "Within 60 days after program end". "Your chosen OPT start date. Once OPT starts, your 12-month clock and unemployment clock begin."
  5. **OPT End Date**: "12 months after OPT start". "Your EAD card expires. You must stop working unless you have filed for STEM extension or have another valid work authorization."

**Friction.** Help sits on a different site with different navigation, so the user loses their place in the dashboard. The guide is general and not linked to the user's own dates.

**What works.** The colour-coded vertical timeline is easy to take in. "This is NOT additional OPT time" pre-empts a common misunderstanding. Using the program end date from the I-20 as the anchor is correct and helpful.

**Accuracy and legal risk.**

- **The filing window leaves out a constraint.** It gives only "90 days before to 60 days after program end" and "Must apply within 60 days of program end date". It does not mention that the application must be filed within 30 days of the DSO's recommendation in SEVIS (8 CFR 214.2(f)(11)(i)(B)). A student who follows this page alone could file late.
- **"Time used reduces post-completion OPT" is incomplete.** Part-time pre-completion OPT counts at half rate (SEVP guidance, 6 months part-time = 3 months). Without this, a student could overcount how much OPT they have used.
- **"OPT End Date: 12 months after OPT start" is not always true.** The EAD end date can be earlier if pre-completion OPT was used. The date printed on the card is the one that counts.
- **The grace-period line has two problems.** "start OPT" within the grace period mixes up choosing a requested start date with the grace period. The line also does not say that a pending OPT application keeps the student in status.
- **"Once OPT starts ... unemployment clock begins" needs a qualifier.** The clock starts on the EAD validity start date, which can be later than the requested start if approval comes late.
- **"You must stop working unless you have filed for STEM extension" is too loose.** Only a *timely-filed* STEM application gives the 180-day extension, and so does a cap-gap situation.
- **There is no date, version or source citation** on any of these rules.

**Screen and state.** The same page, scrolled down. The top shows the end of a green list ("Paid employment related to your field of study", "Self-employment (must be properly documented)", "Working at least 20 hours per week", "Multiple part-time jobs totaling 20+ hours"). Its heading is off-screen, but it is presumably what counts as employment.

**Content.**

- **"STEM OPT Extension (24 Months)"**: "Students with STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics) degrees can apply for an additional 24-month extension, giving you a total of 36 months of OPT."
  - **Eligibility Requirements:**
    - STEM degree from SEVP-certified school
    - Currently employed on post-completion OPT
    - Employer enrolled in E-Verify
    - Job related to STEM degree field
    - Apply up to 90 days before OPT expires
  - **Application Timeline:**
    - Apply up to 90 days before OPT ends
    - Must be received by USCIS before OPT expires
    - Can continue working up to 180 days while pending
    - New I-20 required from DSO
    - Submit Form I-765 with fee
  - **Important Note (amber box):** "You can apply for STEM OPT up to 2 times in your lifetime if you earn multiple qualifying STEM degrees at different education levels (e.g., Bachelor's then Master's)."
- **"Cap Gap Extension"**: "If your employer files an H-1B petition on your behalf that is selected in the lottery, you may be eligible for a "cap gap" extension of your OPT and F-1 status."
  - **How Cap Gap Works:**
    - **"Automatically extends OPT through October 1"**
    - "Only if H-1B petition is timely filed and selected"
    - "EAD card will show expired date but you can still work"
    - "Carry cap-gap approval notice when traveling domestically"
- **"Still Have Questions?"**, "We're here to help! Reach out to our support team." Buttons: "Email Support" and "USCIS Official Site".
- **"Useful External Resources"**, four link tiles: USCIS Case Status, SEVP Portal, I-765 Instructions, STEM Degree List.

**Friction.** Dense text blocks and a wall of checklists. There is no personal "does this apply to me" path.

**What works.** The two-column split between eligibility and timeline is easy to scan. Linking to the official USCIS, SEVP, I-765 and STEM list sources builds trust. "Can continue working up to 180 days while pending" is the reassurance students look for.

**Accuracy and legal risk. These are the most serious findings in this set.**

- **Cap-gap "through October 1" is out of date.** The H-1B modernization final rule (89 FR 103054, FR Doc. 2024-29354, effective 2025-01-17) moved the cap-gap end to **April 1** of the fiscal year, or the petition start date if earlier. Duly's rulepack records this as `capgap.end_month_day = 04-01`, effective 2025-01-17. In September 2026 this page understates cap-gap protection by six months. A student could wrongly believe their status ends on October 1.
- **Cap-gap conditions are loose.** "Only if H-1B petition is timely filed and selected" leaves out that the petition must request a change of status, that the student must be in a valid OPT period or grace period when it is filed, and that the extension ends if the petition is denied, withdrawn or revoked.
- **"Carry cap-gap approval notice when traveling domestically" is unclear.** There is no such standard "cap-gap approval notice"; the usual evidence is an I-20 with a cap-gap update. The line also does not warn that travel abroad generally ends cap-gap.
- **STEM eligibility is incomplete.** "Currently employed on post-completion OPT" is not the regulatory test, which is being in a valid period of post-completion OPT. The list leaves out:
  - Form I-983 (the training plan)
  - the 60-day filing deadline after the DSO recommendation
  - the rule that the job must be paid and at least 20 hours per week
  - that self-employment is not allowed on STEM OPT
  - that a previous STEM degree (within 10 years, from an accredited US school) can qualify
- **"Must be received by USCIS before OPT expires" is a reading, not the rule's words.** The rule's words are "while in a valid period of post-completion OPT", which a cap-gap period can extend.
- **The lifetime-limit note is imprecise.** "different education levels (e.g., Bachelor's then Master's)" is directionally right, but the rule requires the second degree to be at a **higher** level.
- **"Multiple part-time jobs totaling 20+ hours" should be checked against current SEVP guidance.** SEVP allows multiple employers and says all work must relate to the degree. Whether hours from several jobs can be added up for post-completion OPT should be confirmed before Duly states anything similar.

6. **Things they do well that Duly should match or beat, not copy:**
