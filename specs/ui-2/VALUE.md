# ui-2 round 4: the value Duly gives a student

Round 4 goal: give students as much as TrackMyOPT gives them, or more, in Duly's own calm way.
Never copy their design, their words or their clutter. Rounds 1 to 3 of BRIEF.md still hold. Duly
is web, phone and desktop. Needs come from competitor/ANALYSIS.md section 2, gaps from section 6.

## 1. The promise

- **Line:** A private place for your F-1 record, right to the day.
- **Subline:** Your documents, dates and next steps in one place, counted from your own papers,
  with every rule one tap away.
- Shown once on Welcome and once under the Home title, nowhere else. When the H-1B, O-1 and EB-1A
  packs are filled (BACKLOG B5), "F-1 record" becomes "immigration record".

## 2. Three pillars

1. **Right to the day.** Every date is counted by the engine from the student's own documents, in
   their own time zone (D33). One value on every screen. The rule, its source and the day it was
   checked sit one tap away. A number with a missing fact is not shown; the missing fact is asked.
2. **Private by design.** Documents are read on the device (D55) and stored encrypted (D54). No
   ads, no partner deals, no selling data, no outside AI reading papers (D13, D60).
3. **Ahead of what comes next.** The next move with its date, reminders free for good, and rule
   changes explained for this student. Alerted only when it matters.

## 4. Free and paid

- **Free, always:** everything that keeps a date right, warns or stores. The count, every date,
  Next steps, reminders on every channel, documents, export and delete, News with "For you",
  Employer check on the student's own job or offer, processing times, case status (manual now,
  live when B8 lands).
- **Paid, extras only,** priced below TrackMyOPT Pro ($4.17 a month billed yearly, seen
  2026-09-30). Price parked (D8). Candidates for the founder to choose:
  - more encrypted backup space above a generous free allowance;
  - a ready packet for an attorney or a new school: the history, dates and a document index in one
    formatted file (the documents themselves stay in the free export);
  - more than one profile on an account, for example a spouse on F-2.
- **The test for any paid feature:** if a student without it could miss a date, lose a paper or
  act on a wrong number, it is free.
- No trial tricks, no struck-through prices, no "Get Pro" in the sidebar or on content screens.
  The plan lives in Settings only.

## 5. What Duly deliberately does not do

- No job board, job tracker or H-1B sponsor search across employers.
- No resume tools, autofill or saved job-site passwords.
- No ads, banners, deals, referral pages or bundled consultations.
- No selling or sharing of student data; no outside AI reading documents.
- No verdicts: no "Eligible", no "Window Closed", no badge about the student or their employer.
- No streaks, red donuts or trend charts of a count.
- No help that leaves the app, and no marketing mixed into important notices.
