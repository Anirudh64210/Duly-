## Findings

### 1. Blocking: a job never confirmed is counted through today, and nothing is asked
engine/path.py:1985-1995 (`_confirmed`), with rulepacks/f1-us/v2026.09/parameters.yaml:1612.
Input: the test case `on_opt(job())` (OPT card 2026-06-01 to 2027-05-31, job w1 from 2026-07-01
at 40 hours, no `confirmed_on`), today 2026-10-02.
What happens: `unemployment.opt_card.a1` is 30 with no `as_of` and no `waits_on`; the check-in has
`confirmed_on`, `due` and `due_days_away` null; no question about `w1.confirmed_on` or
`checkin.w1`. `work.confirmed_on` declares no `when_absent`, so it is `ask` (SPEC-W2-UNKNOWN
point 2), yet `_confirmed` reads its absence as "confirmed through today". Under point 6 the count
differs between the fills (a past date stops it earlier), so it must be withheld and the field
asked, or the pack must declare `none` with screen words that make the empty field a statement.
As built, a job entered once is counted as holding on every later day and is never checked in
on (G8), since nothing in web/ or api/ writes `confirmed_on` yet. test_w2_records.py:688 asserts
the null check-in but nothing about the count.
spec_point: SPEC-W2-UNKNOWN points 2 and 6; SPEC-W2 point 46 (N3).

### 2. Blocking: the limit day counts past the last confirmed day
engine/path.py:1807.
Input: `on_opt(job(end="2026-08-01", confirmed_on="2026-07-15"))`, today 2026-10-02.
What happens: `unemployment.opt_card.a1` is 30 with `as_of` 2026-07-15 (June only, right under
N3), but `unemployment.opt_card.a1.limit_day` is 2026-09-30, a day in the past, with no
`waits_on`. That date is the 90th day of `counted`, which runs past 2026-07-15 (Aug 2 to today).
The two outputs disagree: a past limit day says the count reached 90, the value says 30. N3 says
counters stop at the last confirmed day; the limit day is a counter's output and does not. The
same case also asks the check-in "checkin.w1" on a job whose end is entered.
spec_point: SPEC-W2 point 46 (N2, N3).

### 3. Blocking: two addresses with no link are both read as the first, and a move is dropped
rulepacks/f1-us/v2026.09/parameters.yaml:1679 and 2169.
Input: complete lists stated, program p1 in progress, `ad1` address since 2025-08-15 and `ad2`
address since 2026-09-25, neither with `previous`.
What happens: no `address.report` window, no question, no conflict. The empty `previous` is
declared `none` with the words "Leave empty if this is the first address in the US on this F-1
record", so two records make that statement and only one can be true: a move happened by
2026-09-25 and its report, by the arithmetic of (f)(17), closes 2026-10-05, three days away. The
record has a conflict, and it is read as no move. CLAUDE.md: a gap or a conflict becomes a
question; SPEC-W2-UNKNOWN point 2 lets `none` stand only where the statement is the person's.
spec_point: SPEC-W2-UNKNOWN point 2; D92; D74 (G17).

### 4. Blocking: a trip whose span does not fit still shows its windows with dates
engine/path.py:1155-1165 (the windows are built after the clash sets the span to null).
Input: `on_opt(job(confirmed_on="2026-10-02"), trip(left_on="2026-09-20", returned_on="2026-09-01", signature_on="2026-06-01", purpose="personal", job_on_return="yes"))`.
What happens: stage `trip.t1` is `unknown` with `waits_on` `trip.t1.ends_before_starts`, and the
conflict `span_order.t1` is asked, but `trip.absence_limit.t1` still shows opens 2026-09-20,
closes 2027-02-20 with no `waits_on`. Either day may be the wrong one, so the closing day rests on
a value the record contradicts. Point 32a: the stage's dates are null and it waits on the
conflict. The code path predates W2-P2; the trip windows added here are what make it show.
spec_point: SPEC-W2-UNKNOWN point 32a.
