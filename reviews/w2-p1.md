`engine/readers.py:47` (`as_number` takes any finite number) with `engine/path.py:1924`.

Input (TMPDIR/rvw2h/q3.py): masters p1 ending 2026-05-15, OPT a1 2026-06-01 to 2027-05-31, w1
under a1 from 2026-07-01 with employer e1 and `hours_per_week: -5`. What happens:
`unemployment.opt_card.a1` is 121, its limit day 2026-08-29, and the reason
`unemployment.opt_card.at_or_over_limit.a1` is raised. Nothing asks about w1's hours. With 40
the count is 30, so the count, the limit day and the at-or-over line all rest on a figure no
one can work. A `count` field already refuses a negative (`_count`, readers.py:53); a number
field that holds hours could carry a minimum of 0 in its declaration, or the reader could treat
a negative as unreadable, so it is asked like "forty" is. No test enters a negative hours
figure. This is the third review's finding 4 at one more input; it is not blocking because no
SPEC point or source names the input, and the web form is the only place a person types it.
