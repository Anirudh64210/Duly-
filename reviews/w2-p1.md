## Findings

### 1. Blocking: a gap in one job removes another job's known deadline, and nothing names that job
`engine/path.py:400` (`_select`) with `engine/path.py:616` (`run_stages`).
`_select` returns None for the whole selection when the where condition is unknown for any one
record. `run_stages` now asks for what was read, but it still runs `for record in found or []`,
so every record the selection knew about is dropped with it.

- Input S1: the holdout's `_stem_case("e1")` (STEM card a2 with employer e1 from 2026-07-01; w2
  with e2 from 2026-08-03), plus `w3`: work under a2 from 2026-09-01 with no employer. Through
  `POST /v1/compute`: no stem_job_start stage at all. w2's new Form I-983 window and report
  window, 2026-08-03 to 2026-08-13, are gone, though every fact they need is entered. The only
  reason is `missing:w3.employer`. Nothing names w2 or says its dates are withheld.
- Input O1: post-completion OPT card a1, w1 under a1 from 2026-07-01 (report window 2026-07-01
  to 2026-07-11), plus w2 with no `under`. The opt_job_start stage vanishes, w1's window with it.
  The only question is `missing:w2.under`.
- Input S2: as S1, but w3 has employer e2 and start "2026-13-40". The case contract takes any
  string, and the API answers 200. w2's two windows are gone, and no reason or question names w3
  or w2. `_compare_dates` gives None for the unreadable date, and the trace holds nothing to
  ask, so `_need` asks nothing. The same happens with "08/03/2026".

Before this round all three dropped the same way (checked on a 75cbf7f copy), so this is the
first review's finding 1 fixed for one input and not for the class. The mission says a gap never
silently removes a deadline. Here w2's 10-day deadline from (f)(10)(ii)(C)(7)(iv) and
(f)(12)(ii)(A) is removed because of another record, and nothing about w2 says why (SPEC-W2 45
P3, PRINCIPLES 2). The fix is generic: let `_select` keep the records it knows and report the
undecided ones, so run_stages enters the known ones and asks about the rest. A selection that is
unknown with nothing to ask, such as an unreadable date, needs a reason too. No test has two
records under one selection with one unknown.

parses contracts/product_settings.yaml again. Load it once in `validate_pack` and pass it down,
or cache it with `functools.cache`.
